Gailey Road's Arts-Based Research Skill-Building Workshop
June 23-26, 2025

Thirty participants joined Gailey Road's 4-day workshop to develop arts-based research skills in:

  • Verbatim Script Writing
  • Song Writing
  • Costume Design

The theme of workshop was Building Queer and Trans Identities, Communities, and Allyship. Participants spent 4 days learning the skills of verbatim script writing, song writing and costume design and then presented their work-in-progress on the last day of the workshop.

A report that shares what participant experiences in the workshop and what they learned will be available soon.
