La Casa De Harriet 2014

Playwright and Producer: Tara Goldstein

Director and translator: Jorge Arcila

Assistant Director: Claudia Muñoz

In July 2014, Gailey Road brought La Casa de Harriet (Harriet’s House) to Bogotá, Colombia. Tara Goldstein and the company produced.

  1. A performance of La Casa de Harriet (Harriet’s House) at La Festival Rosa was hosted by Barraca Teatro in Bogotá, Colombia, Thursday, July 24, 2014.
  2. A workshop about the experiences of adoptive same-sex families in schools using scenes from La Casa de Harriet for 80 teachers from the Bogotá District School Board, Friday, July 25, 2014. The workshop was hosted by IDEP (the Research Institute for Pedagogical and Educational Development which is part of the School Board) in Bogotá, Colombia.
  3. Performance of La Casa de Harriet at the theatre at the Universidad Distrital Franciso José de Caldas in Bogotá, Colombia on Friday July 25, 2014.
  4. Performance of La Casa de Harriet (Harriet’s House) for the Bogotá District School Board, hosted by IDEP Tuesday, July 29, 2014.
